# LegalEase: Intelligent Office & Appointment Management System

The LegalEase Appointment and Office Management System is a web-based legal service platform that digitizes the operations of a legal and notary office. It allows clients to register with email verification, book appointments with real-time slot availability, and communicate with staff through a role-based interface supporting Admin, Staff, and Client users. The system handles payment processing with discount computation and receipt emailing, refund management, feedback collection with testimonial curation, and internal messaging. An AI-powered chatbot using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline answers client questions about services, availability, and office procedures in English, Filipino, and Taglish, with real-time streaming responses and the ability to execute actions like booking appointments.
